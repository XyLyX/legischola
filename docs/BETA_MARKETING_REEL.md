## Suggested voiceover

Law school is demanding. Syllabi, cases, notes, deadlines and exams can leave students wondering what to study next.

Meet LegiSchola: an AI-powered companion designed to make legal learning more connected.

Explore your semester, ask questions, organise Cornell notes, and practise thinking through legal problems—not just memorising answers.

Use knowledge checks, timed mock exams and adaptive revision to discover what needs attention.

We're starting with a public beta, learning from students as we improve.

LegiSchola. Learn the law. Learn to think legally.
