# LegiSchola public academic publishing policy

## Source of truth
- University-issued syllabi and notices take precedence over secondary summaries.
- Preserve university, programme, academic year, semester, source URL/document, verification date, and reviewer identity.
- Do not publish subject/unit claims as verified without checking the applicable official source.
- Distinguish platform-generated learning aids from university-authored curriculum and past papers.
- No student profile, notes, scores, guardian information, or account records may appear on public routes.

## Publication gate
1. Draft from source material with traceable references.
2. Academic reviewer checks every syllabus claim, edition and programme mapping.
3. Editorial reviewer checks originality, clarity, misleading claims and link targets.
4. Run build, link, schema, canonical, sitemap and robots checks.
5. Publish only after review. Recheck when a university issues syllabus changes.

## SEO/GEO quality
- Write original explanations answering actual student questions; do not mass-generate near-duplicate pages.
- Provide concise definitions, scope, limitations, and primary-source links where applicable.
- Avoid claims of official university affiliation, guaranteed grades, or guaranteed AI search placement.
- Structured data must describe visible page content accurately.
- No paid search or conversion tracking is activated by this policy.

## Current release
Public semester pages are explicitly generic until verified subject-level content is available. Do not silently infer subjects from other cohorts or academic years.
